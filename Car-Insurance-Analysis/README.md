# 🚗 Car Insurance Risk & Claim Analysis Dashboard Using Python

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Car Insurance Risk & Claim Analysis Dashboard** developed using **Python**.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of insurance policy data and helps analyze:

![HR Dashboard Preview](https://github.com/Adityakaushik-mrt/Python/blob/main/Car-Insurance-Analysis/Fianl%20Dashboard%20using%20Python.PNG?raw=true)

- Insurance policy distribution
- Claim amounts
- Claim frequency
- Car usage
- Car makes
- Coverage zones
- Age groups
- Car year trends
- Education
- Marital status
- Kids driving patterns

The dashboard also includes interactive filters for **Gender** and **Measure**, allowing users to explore the data dynamically.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to transform car insurance data into meaningful business insights through data analysis and visualization.

The dashboard helps answer questions such as:

- How many insurance policies are available?
- What is the total claim amount?
- What is the average claim amount?
- What is the average claim frequency?
- Which car makes have the highest number of policies?
- How are policies distributed across different car-use categories?
- Which coverage zones have more policies?
- How are policies distributed across different age groups?
- How does the number of policies change by car year?
- What is the relationship between education and insurance policies?
- How does claim amount vary according to education and marital status?
- How does kids-driving behavior appear in the policy data?

## 📈 Key KPIs

The dashboard displays important insurance KPIs including:

### Total Policies
Displays the overall policy value based on the selected measure.

### Total Claim Amount
Shows the total claim amount associated with the insurance policies.

### Average Claim Amount
Calculates the average claim amount.

### Average Claim Frequency
Displays the average frequency of insurance claims.
